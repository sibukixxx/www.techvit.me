A system that automates business workflows through generative AI and API integrations, with an initial focus on handling customer inquiries.

```text
Inquiry
  ↓
AI classification
  ↓
Knowledge retrieval
  ↓
Response generation
  ↓
Human escalation
```

The goal is not to automate every decision. The system preserves human involvement wherever judgment, accountability, or exception handling is required.
