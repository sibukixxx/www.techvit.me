Small Manufacturer Hub brings together free tools for purchasing and inventory decisions with comparisons of related SaaS products for small manufacturers.

## Core features

- Reorder Point Calculator
- Safety Stock Calculator
- EOQ Calculator
- SaaS comparison content
- Affiliate monetization

It is also a practical experiment in combining engineering, SEO, and affiliate business models to build a sustainable niche product.
