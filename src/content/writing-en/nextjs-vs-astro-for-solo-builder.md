---
title: Why I Choose Astro over Next.js for Solo Projects
description: For sites that do not require continuous SSR, Astro, Content Collections, and Cloudflare Pages can reduce maintenance cost while improving speed.
category: engineering
tags: [Astro, Cloudflare, Next.js]
pubDate: 2026-06-01
---

For personal sites and small product websites without a complex backend, a primarily static architecture often provides lower maintenance costs and better performance.

With Astro, Content Collections, and Cloudflare Pages, only the features that need dynamic processing have to be implemented as Pages Functions. This can satisfy the requirements without adopting an architecture like Next.js that assumes continuous server-side rendering.
