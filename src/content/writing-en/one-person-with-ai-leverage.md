There is frequent discussion about AI reducing the need for engineers, but my practical experience points in another direction. Writing code with AI will increasingly become a commodity. Deciding what should be built, whether it works correctly, and whether it can become a viable business will remain essential work.

Alongside client engineering, I am using AI to operate several small businesses independently. This section records what I learn during that transition.
